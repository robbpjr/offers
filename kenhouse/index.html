CONFIDENTIAL NON-BINDING PROPOSAL

Dear Ken,

Rob and Brandon are pleased to submit this non-binding proposal to acquire Ken’s House, including all related garage rights, couch capacity, backyard optionality, refrigerator access, snack inventory, and future hangout cash flows.

We believe Ken’s House represents a highly strategic residential platform with premium neighborhood positioning, underutilized patio infrastructure, and significant long-term social upside.

Strategic Rationale

Rob and Brandon believe the combination of Ken’s House with our existing social operations would create a category-leading platform for:

  • Grilling
  • Game watching
  • Golf commentary
  • Garage-based storage optimization
  • Light beverage distribution
  • Weekend hangout scalability

We see meaningful opportunities to improve operational efficiency through centralized Costco procurement, shared streaming subscriptions, coordinated lawn chair deployment, and enhanced refrigerator inventory management.

Additional Strategic Assets

The buyer group recognizes the significant intangible value associated with Ken’s existing plumeria flower photo portfolio, including but not limited to:

  • Golden hour backyard plumeria photography rights
  • Seasonal bloom appreciation upside
  • Instagram-compatible tropical aesthetics
  • Long-term fragrance and vibe generation potential
  • Potential future licensing opportunities for “calm backyard energy” branding initiatives

Rob and Brandon believe the plumeria assets remain materially undervalued by current neighborhood market participants.

Governance

Following closing, Rob would serve as Executive Chairman of Outdoor Activities and Strategic Beverage Operations.

Brandon would serve as Head of Infrastructure, Music Queue Management, and Vibe Continuity.

Conclusion

This proposal is non-binding and intended solely as the basis for further discussion.

We look forward to working constructively with Ken and his advisors to complete this transformative transaction.
